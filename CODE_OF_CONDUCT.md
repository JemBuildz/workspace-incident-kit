# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful place to make troubleshooting safer and easier. Be kind, constructive, and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people. Welcome newcomers and different levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing another person’s private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer. Do not include real captures, credentials, cookies, or customer data in a report.
